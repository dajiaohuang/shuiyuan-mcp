# Shuiyuan Rules Guardrails

Use this reference before publishing public Shuiyuan topics or replies, especially when content mentions people, disputes, screenshots, private messages, current events, or reposted material.

Primary sources read from Shuiyuan:

- Shuiyuan Community Guidelines: https://shuiyuan.sjtu.edu.cn/faq
- Shuiyuan Terms of Service: https://shuiyuan.sjtu.edu.cn/tos
- New user FAQ: https://shuiyuan.sjtu.edu.cn/t/topic/279293/1
- New user feature guide: https://shuiyuan.sjtu.edu.cn/t/topic/279294/1
- Notice on doxxing / callout behavior: https://shuiyuan.sjtu.edu.cn/t/topic/324397/1

## Posting Checklist

Before posting:

1. Search first and avoid duplicate topics. The ToS recommends using search and the editor's similar-topic hints before creating a topic.
2. Choose an appropriate category and tags. Wrong-category or off-topic content may be moved, hidden, split, locked, or deleted.
3. Keep the title accurate, non-clickbait, and within 48 characters.
4. Keep the post relevant to the category and topic. Create a new topic if the reply would derail the current topic.
5. Prefer links over screenshots when sharing Shuiyuan content externally or referencing other Shuiyuan posts.

## Content To Avoid

Do not publish or assist with content that:

- Violates laws, school rules, public order, or public morals.
- Uses exaggerated or misleading titles, hides key details, distorts facts, mismatches text/images, quotes out of context, or uses ambiguous abbreviations/near-homophones to mislead.
- Reposts news or third-party material without clear source attribution when required by the category.
- Publishes advertising outside advertising categories, or disguises ads as tutorials, guides, or experience posts.
- Contains harassment, insults, mockery, identity-based attacks, rumor-mongering, or content that damages another person's reputation.
- Incites conflict, discrimination, extreme emotion, or attacks a specific identity or group.
- Includes malware, phishing links, malicious scripts, exploit instructions, or instructions to disrupt Shuiyuan or other services.
- Contains unlabelled AI/deep-learning generated text, image, audio, video, or virtual scenes when the synthetic nature matters.

## Privacy And Screenshots

Be strict with privacy:

- Do not publish names, identities, photos, contact details, chat records, Shuiyuan private messages, or other personal information without the person's consent.
- Shuiyuan treats unauthorized publication of chat records and private messages as privacy-sensitive even when copied as text.
- Public-interest or self-protection exceptions require reasonable handling of personal information; anonymize and minimize details.
- Do not "open-box" / doxx people, call people out with private identifying details, or encourage others to do so.
- Do not publish Shuiyuan content outside Shuiyuan as images or videos. The guidelines classify image/video reposting outside the community as malicious behavior. Use Shuiyuan topic/post links instead.

## Automation And Request Safety

The ToS restricts unofficial clients, scripts, crawlers, and high-frequency automated access. When using Shuiyuan MCP:

- Use the user's own authenticated session only for the user's requested task.
- Keep reads targeted. Search first, then read only relevant topics.
- Prefer raw long-topic reads and narrow structured windows instead of bulk per-post crawling.
- Avoid high-frequency posting, replying, private messaging, reactions, reports, or bulk scraping.
- Never expose cookies, session data, email addresses, or account identifiers.

## Handling Disputes Or Sensitive Events

For event summaries, controversy, or accusations:

- Distinguish official/primary sources, party statements, and community interpretation.
- Avoid presenting rumors or corrected claims as fact.
- Link to source posts instead of copying large chunks or screenshots.
- Remove or anonymize unnecessary personal identifiers.
- Use neutral wording and include uncertainty where Shuiyuan sources disagree.

## Reporting And Feedback

For questionable content, use Shuiyuan's report flow or contact `@版主` with necessary evidence. For policy or site suggestions, use `站点反馈` (`category_id: 2`).
